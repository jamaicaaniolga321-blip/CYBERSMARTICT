Cybersecurity awareness

Think before you click.

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting devices, accounts, networks, and information from digital threats. ICT students can use these skills in school, at home, and in future technology careers.

🎣

Spot phishing

Learn to notice suspicious messages, fake login pages, and links that ask for private information.

🔐

Protect accounts

Use long, unique passwords and turn on multi-factor authentication when available.

🛡️

Keep data private

Share less personal information and review app permissions and privacy settings.

Cybersecurity lessons

Read each lesson and remember the key action.

🎣

1. Phishing

Phishing is a trick that uses fake emails, messages, or websites to make people reveal information or click unsafe links.

Remember: Check the sender and website address. If unsure, don't click; verify through an official channel.
🔑

2. Strong passwords

A strong password is long and difficult to guess. Avoid names, birthdays, and reusing the same password on many accounts.

Remember: Use a unique password for every important account and a trusted password manager if available.
📲

3. Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds another verification step beyond a password.

Remember: Turn it on when available. Never share verification codes with someone who asks unexpectedly.
🧑‍💻

4. Privacy and personal data

Personal data includes details such as your address, phone number, school information, and account details.

Remember: Share only what is necessary and check who can see your posts and profile.
🔗

5. Safe browsing

Scammers may use urgent messages, unexpected prizes, or unusual links to pressure people into acting quickly.

Remember: Pause, check the source, and visit official websites by typing the address yourself.
🔄

6. Updates and downloads

Software updates can fix security weaknesses. Unknown downloads may contain harmful software.

Remember: Update devices and apps, and download programs only from trusted sources.
If something seems wrong: Don't reply or forward a suspicious message. Tell a trusted adult, teacher, or school IT staff member, and use the service's official reporting tools.

About this project

Project title: Development and Effectiveness of a Cybersecurity Education and Awareness Website for ICT Students

Target users: ICT students

Purpose: To provide basic cybersecurity lessons and assess whether using the website is associated with improved cybersecurity knowledge.

Suggested evaluation: A pre-test and post-test, plus a short usability feedback form.

Research reminder

Ask your teacher for approval before testing participants. Explain the study, get any required consent, keep results private, and do not collect passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information. Report actual results honestly; do not claim the website is effective until the data has been analyzed.

This is an educational student project, not a complete cybersecurity protection tool or professional security audit.
